# Sea Sparrow MVP Plan (condensed)

Scope IN: one ocean arena, player corvette, 3 enemy corvettes, main gun (ballistic),
homing missile, simple AI, HP/damage, explosions/splash, HUD + minimap, win/lose.
Scope OUT: customization, fleet orders, submarines, weather system, campaign,
multiplayer, FFT ocean, full damage compartments.

Phases
 1 Greybox scene + ship movement + chase camera
 2 Weapons + hit detection + FX
 3 Enemy AI + win/lose + HUD
 4 Art pass: replace greybox with blockout models, ocean texture, lighting
 5 Polish: shake, tracers, sound hooks, balance

Acceptance
  Ship accelerates/turns with inertia; camera never clips sea.
  Gun shells show arc + splash; hits register.
  Enemies approach, circle, fire; die with explosion.
  Desktop 1080p ~60fps.
